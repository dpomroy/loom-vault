# Small Teams, Clear Principles, Delegated Authority

These sources converge on a counterintuitive idea: the best leadership is largely about creating conditions, not exercising control. Koch's principle-based management aims for a culture where people know what to do without being told. Kelly Johnson operationalized this with brutal headcount restrictions — a small number of good people, minimal bureaucracy. Ross at Groq frames it as a hiring philosophy: don't over-constrain the goal, so your team can surprise you. Catmull at Pixar puts it most concretely — his job was managing room dynamics, not making the movies. Where they differ is in structure: Johnson preferred near-autocratic delegation to one person, while Catmull and Koch emphasize culture and principles as the coordinating mechanism. Ross sits between them, using under-specification as a leadership tool.
