# Safety Branding as Competitive Weapon

Multiple sources converge on a damning structural critique: AI safety rhetoric has become the primary instrument of market consolidation. The argument runs from different angles but lands in the same place. One source frames it as explicit strategy — brand as safe, ban competitors, profit. Another exposes the logical trap embedded in Anthropic's framework: any safety architecture requiring a single trusted key-holder will always output its own author as that holder. Sam Altman, notably a competitor, voices the same fear from the opposite direction — not that safety is fake, but that it becomes a gatekeeping mechanism. Where sources differ is tone: some treat this as cynical calculation, others as genuine but self-serving rationalization. The critique doesn't require bad faith to land.
