# AI's Existential Business Trap: Disrupting Yourself to Survive

These sources collectively identify the same structural tension: the companies that built AI's foundations are most threatened by its success. The shift from sustaining to disruptive innovation happened overnight with ChatGPT, forcing Google to cannibalize its $140B Search machine to stay relevant. Bill Maris adds a sharper competitive edge — Google could theoretically crush OpenAI and Anthropic simply by slashing token prices, which reveals how precarious the business models of frontier labs actually are. But Sam Altman flips the frame entirely: his fear isn't commercial disruption, it's regulatory capture — that 'too dangerous' becomes the justification for locking AI to a small group. Together, these sources suggest the real battle isn't technical capability but who controls access and who bears the cost of disruption.
