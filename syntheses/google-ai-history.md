# Google Built AI's Foundation, Then Froze

These sources collectively tell a story of a company that won every technological era it competed in — and then hesitated at the threshold of the one it created. Google survived the web-to-mobile transition that destroyed IBM and Microsoft's dominance, yet stumbled on AI despite inventing the Transformer. The tension is specific: publishing 'Attention Is All You Need' was simultaneously a gift to humanity and a self-inflicted wound. When ChatGPT reframed AI as a disruptive rather than sustaining innovation, Google's search revenue became a liability, not just an asset. Its unique position — owning both frontier models and custom AI chips — may be its best defense, but only if it can move against its own business model.
