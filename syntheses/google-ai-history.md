# Google Built AI's Foundation, Then Hesitated

These sources collectively tell a story of Google as the singular architect of modern AI — responsible for the talent, the infrastructure, and the core breakthrough — who then fumbled the moment of disruption. Google employed virtually every foundational AI researcher, and its teams invented the Transformer, the architecture powering all frontier models today. Yet the decision to publish 'Attention Is All You Need' openly handed competitors the blueprint. When ChatGPT converted that blueprint into a consumer product, Google was caught flat-footed. The sharpest tension across sources: Google's current AI position is uniquely strong — owning both frontier models and custom chips — but its $140B search business creates a structural incentive to slow-walk the very disruption it originally caused.
