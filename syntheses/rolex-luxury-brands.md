# Scarcity, Myth, and Identity Define Luxury Brands

These sources converge on a striking idea: luxury brands like Rolex and Ferrari don't just sell products — they sell identity and myth, and scarcity is the mechanism that makes both possible. The Swiss watch industry's survival required a deliberate inversion: turning mechanical watchmaking's obsolescence into an artisanal virtue. Ferrari runs the same play differently — withholding supply to sustain desire rather than repositioning an old technology. Where they diverge is in what drives the purchase. Ferrari buyers are chasing exclusivity and racing mythology. Rolex buyers, as the Apple Watch era clarified, are seeking a signal about who they are when nothing functional compels them to wear a watch at all.
