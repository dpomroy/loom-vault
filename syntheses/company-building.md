# Small Teams, High Density, Delegated Authority

These sources converge on a counterintuitive principle: organizational effectiveness comes from compression, not expansion. Koch's vision of a culture where people know what to do without being told, Kelly Johnson's near-vicious restriction of headcount, and Anthropic's explicit preference for talent density over talent mass all point the same direction — fewer, better people operating with clear principles outperform large coordinated groups. Spotify's observation that org charts eventually show up in products adds a structural warning: how you organize isn't just an internal matter, it shapes what you can build. Where they differ is in mechanism — Koch relies on internalized principles, Johnson on centralized authority, Anthropic on selective hiring, and Söderström on long-tenured trust.
